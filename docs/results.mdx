## Legal contracts, DeepSeek, 140 questions

Corpus: 468 contracts and privacy policies from LegalBench-RAG (CUAD, ContractNLI, PrivacyQA), split into 13,962 passages.

| Policy | Snippet hit | Char recall | Char precision | Hops | p50 | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fine, 1 passage per hop | 0.469 | 0.423 | 0.120 | 2.84 | 8.9 s | $0.063 |
| Coarse, 4 passages per hop | **0.575** | **0.547** | 0.065 | **1.51** | **4.2 s** | **$0.047** |

Coarse minus fine, paired bootstrap at 95%:

- Snippet hit **+0.106** (+0.046 to +0.166)
- Character recall **+0.124** (+0.060 to +0.186)
- Character precision **-0.055** (-0.082 to -0.031)

## Legal contracts, MiMo, 100 questions

| Policy | Snippet hit | Char recall | Char precision | Hops | p50 | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fine | 0.509 | 0.441 | 0.129 | 2.90 | 15.7 s | $0.061 |
| Coarse | 0.562 | **0.535** | 0.064 | 1.59 | 8.6 s | $0.050 |

- Recall gain **+0.094** (+0.023 to +0.166), significant.
- Snippet-hit gain **+0.053** (-0.015 to +0.123), not distinguishable from zero.

## What this means in practice

Fetching more per hop finds evidence more often and finishes sooner, because the agent needs fewer hops. It returns more text that is not the answer, so a pipeline that re-ranks or trims the context before generation will keep the gain and pay less for it.

The earlier HotpotQA study, which measured answer quality on a benchmark that has gold answers, is in the repository and the live report.
